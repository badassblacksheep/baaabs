# BAAABS

The official website for BAAABS—the Bad Ass Amazingly Awesome Black Sheep.

BAAABS is a towering, fire-eyed black sheep built around Earl, a 2007 Thomas HDX rear-engine school bus. The project combines a rooftop dance floor, sculptural panel skin, sound, and fire effects.

Website: <https://baaabs.org/>  
Repository: <https://github.com/badassblacksheep/baaabs>

## Current structural direction

The former **subframe station** concept (including six paired stations, cantilevered arms, and station-based mounts) has been abandoned as the active design direction. Historical studies and assets may remain in the repository for reference; they are **not fabrication instructions**.

The current concept is a **36 ft × 8 ft rooftop deck**, with a cross-braced square-tube steel frame and plywood floor. Two alternative chassis-connected support layouts are under study:

1. One upright attached to the driver-side chassis rail.
2. Four uprights arranged as two transverse pairs, with chassis connections to be engineered.

Neither configuration is approved for fabrication. The load path, attachment geometry, vehicle-frame interface, local reinforcement, member sizing, welds, fasteners, torsion, and dynamic loads require verification. A physical survey of Earl is required before finalizing the support layout and bill of materials.

Follow the live structural study and survey deliverables at <https://baaabs.org/structural>. Consult `AGENTS.md` for contributor instructions.

## Road configuration

The former project-specific **hard 102-inch road-width invariant** and its station-derived width checks are retired as design requirements. This does **not** waive applicable vehicle width laws, permit requirements, or safe transport clearances. The roadworthy configuration must be measured and evaluated separately before travel. Historical width-check scripts and reports may remain for reference, but must not be presented as the current structural design gate.

## Exterior panels and model

The panel workshop at <https://baaabs.org/the_build.html> tracks **206 exterior panels**. Preserve the sheep's overall appearance, panel count, and symmetry where practical while updating the deck and structural model.

The repository contains historical model revisions, mounting concepts, and subframe studies. Older documentation and geometry depicting stations, six-station frames, or 102-inch project gates are archival, not authoritative descriptions of the current proposal.

Supplier: McNICHOLS, Fife, Washington. Reference pattern: https://www.mcnichols.com/perforated-metal/round-hole/carbon-steel-cs-16183122 (verify the exact 20-gauge SKU and availability before ordering; linked listing may be a different gauge).

Use this as the **working estimate for all 206 nonstructural exterior panels**, not for the roof, chassis supports, guardrails, or load-bearing components. Approximate bare sheet weight: 0.88 lb/ft² of gross sheet area at 40% open area; excludes edging, frames, mounts, coating, and offcuts. Actual weight depends on mill thickness and perforation pattern. Confirm final area and nesting before budgeting or ordering.

Do not treat this tentative choice as a verified specification or purchase order.

## Exterior skin mass study

Earl’s 206 detachable panels store flat. Each installed surface and its cut pattern are convex, with 139 triangles, 58 quadrilaterals, 7 pentagons and 2 hexagons. The six hoof plates and all three circular cutouts retain their original geometry. B-D-031 replaces the narrow upper-flank fan above the DJ window. B-D-085 is a separate tapered strip beside the original opening. [Inspect the upper flank](https://baaabs.org/the_build.html#B-D-031) or [the window strip](https://baaabs.org/the_build.html#B-D-085).

To keep the count at 206, the body has open skin at roof crowns 01, 02, 04, 05 and 07 on each side. These ten skins cover 11.11 m² in the previous revision. The fitted shell contains 183.33 m² (1,973.33 ft²) of sheet. The fabrication register records every sheet in its own plane. The largest modeled joint adjustment is 8.4 mm. Redrawing the passenger-side B-P-060/070 diagonal changes the interior of that facet pair by roughly 43 mm while retaining the outer boundary of the pair before the joint fit.

Every pattern fits one 60 × 120 in sheet with at least 0.86 in of margin per side when centered in its listed orientation. The heaviest 20-gauge steel skin is 26.9 lb. Allowing 0.50 lb/ft of edging and 2 lb of fittings gives a peak estimated assembly weight of 41.0 lb. Check finished handling on Earl. [Panel materials and handling estimates](https://baaabs.org/panels.html).
