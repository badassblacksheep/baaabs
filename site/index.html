What is BAAABS?

An Ovine Dancefloor with IBS.

BAAABS is a monumental mobile artwork: a faceted black sheep with a welded steel frame and perforated aluminum skin, lit from within and carried on the bones of a school bus.

Current status

Right now: hunting for the bus.

BAAABS currently exists as a vision, a design, and an increasingly detailed plan. I’m now actively seeking the full-size school bus that will become her bones.

Once it’s mine, Phase One begins: structure, skin, light, fire, rooftop dance floor, controls, safety systems, and the approvals required to roll onto the playa.

The bus we’re seeking

Full-size bus. Full-width ambition.

We’re looking for a structurally sound, full-size school bus in the same heavy-duty class as this 2011 Blue Bird All American. A rear-engine transit-style bus or a conventional full-size bus can work. What matters most is a diesel drivetrain, dual rear wheels, air brakes, a robust chassis, obtainable parts, and enough verified payload capacity to become the bones of BAAABS. The exact badge, nose, length, seating layout, and paint are secondary to condition and capability.

Inside, we expect to remove nearly every passenger seat while probably keeping the rear row. That leaves an open, adaptable volume for equipment, storage, circulation, and whatever the build ultimately teaches us we need. An ADA lift is not on the wish list; we would rather avoid the weight, space, maintenance, and body opening unless an otherwise exceptional bus makes a compelling case for keeping or thoughtfully removing one.

The scale is deliberate. In her finished art-car configuration, BAAABS is being designed for deep-playa operation rather than ordinary street use. We accept the turning radius, clearance, storage, transport, setup, and access constraints that come with building her at full size; the operating plan will be shaped around the artwork instead of shrinking the artwork around the operating plan.

The transformation

One practical layer at a time, a bus becomes a black sheep built to survive dust, dancing, and terrible ideas executed well.

  1. 01

    Bones

    A roadworthy bus and welded steel structure give the sheep her stance, strength, and unmistakable silhouette.

  2. 02

    Skin

    Black perforated aluminum panels turn the frame into a shifting field of facets—solid by day, porous after dark.

  3. 03

    Spark

    Light pours through the perforations. Animated light brings her eyes to life. The rooftop becomes a dance floor beneath the open sky.

  4. 04

    Voice

    A properly engineered sound system completes the transformation and gives BAAABS a voice worthy of the outfit.

The skin we have in mind

Black metal.
Light through the holes.

Flat sheets of perforated aluminum will become the sheep’s angular panels, mounted over a welded steel frame. The staggered round holes let the lighting inside show through the black finish.

The current candidate is 3003-H14 aluminum with approximately 33% open area. The photo shows the perforation pattern; the black finish comes later.
