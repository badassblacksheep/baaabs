Station zones to discuss

Five candidate zones—not fixed locations or a member count.

  1. Front / behind cab
    Head support; keep driver visibility and the passenger entrance clear.
  2. Behind front axle
    Front deck support; work around the front speaker station.
  3. Middle bay
    Shorten deck spans; check equipment and underfloor access.
  4. Ahead of rear axle
    Rear deck support; work around the rear speaker station.
  5. Rear bay
    Rump and stair interface; preserve engine service and equipment access.

The left/right upright pair at each station must connect through a verified chassis attachment. Actual crossmembers, axle travel and service clearances need inspection before positions are fixed.

No new structural members or attachments have been designed here. Existing deck, rails and equipment mounts are concept geometry, not load-rated fabrication details. The finished v16 model is preserved.
