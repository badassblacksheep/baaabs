Where the estimate comes from

The auction does not provide empty weight or weight ratings. We are assuming a 34,200 lb GVWR, a 22,000 lb full-fluid bus with seats, and 2,000 lb removed: 20,000 lb stripped and 14,200 lb available. The 12,000–16,000 lb range is a planning judgment, not a measured range for this VIN.

For context, a different 2012 All American D3 RE in a federal test weighed approximately 20,384 lb unloaded with full fluids and its seats installed; its GVWR was about 33,000 lb. It carried 47 students plus a driver and is not our donor’s configuration. Period Blue Bird literature advertised ratings up to 36,200 lb; that maximum is not assumed for our bus.
