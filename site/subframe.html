Generator exhaust concept: the reference Onan flange connects directly to the existing outlet through thin-wall exhaust tubing. No hood or fan. Rental fit and routing remain provisional. The retained flush outlet needs revision before installation: Cummins calls for at least 1 inch beyond the vehicle perimeter.

Passenger entrance: B-P-050 restored beside the door with a vertical rear jamb. B-P-052 stays removed. The opening now matches the modeled 1 m door width, with a 10-inch step-over edge. Sheet panels are not step treads.

Five provisional stations

Distances run aft from the front bumper. Uprights are 2.60 m apart, just outside the bus sides. Each pair has a lower crossbearer and a removable transverse header above the roof.
