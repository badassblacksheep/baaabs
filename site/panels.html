Two-person handling study

Every developed pattern fits on one 60 × 120 in sheet. Across the register the largest stock length is 115.65 in and the largest stock width is 58.51 in, on different pieces. In their listed orientations the tightest centered pattern has 0.74 in of stock left on each limiting edge. The longest installed corner-to-corner span is 124.9 in. The workshop marks formed creases on 14 single-piece patterns.

Weights use assumed perimeter hardware. Two-person handling also depends on grip points, wind and the carry route. Test a finished full-size piece with two crew members before releasing cut files. Final patterns need the chosen bend radius, bend allowance, fastener holes and cutting clearances.

Procurement and qualification

Request a 20-gauge sample and bulk quotation; validate the pattern, flame proximity, edge reinforcement, corrosion protection, highway wind loads, oil-canning, vibration and mounting fatigue on a representative large panel. Calculate full-sheet procurement quantities from a nesting plan before purchase. The exterior skin is nonstructural; it does not carry deck loads.
