Exterior panels · material study

BAAABS has 206 modeled exterior skin panels. This page tracks skin material selection, weight, procurement and validation separately from the interactive panel workshop.

Working candidate — not approved for fabrication

McNICHOLS 20-gauge perforated carbon steel, nominal 0.0359 in (0.91 mm), 1/8 in round holes on 3/16 in staggered centers, approximately 40% open area. Verify exact 20-gauge product SKU, availability and quotation with the supplier in Fife, WA. Supplier pattern reference (linked listing may be a different gauge).

Model-based skin mass

The current 206-panel register sums to 193.72 m² = 2,085.18 ft² net modeled surface area. At 40% open area:

MaterialEstimated bare skin
Existing 0.063 in perforated aluminum~1,107 lb
Provisional 20 ga perforated steel~1,833 lb

Important: the proposed steel is ~726 lb heavier than the existing aluminum assumption, These estimates exclude frame members, edging, fasteners, coatings, scrap and sheet nesting. Thickness, actual open area and stock properties must be confirmed.

Procurement and qualification

Request a 20-gauge sample and bulk quotation; validate the pattern, flame proximity, edge reinforcement, corrosion protection, highway wind loads, oil-canning, vibration and mounting fatigue on a representative large panel. Calculate full-sheet procurement quantities from a nesting plan before purchase. The exterior skin is nonstructural; it does not carry deck loads.
