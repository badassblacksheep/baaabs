Earl’s roof frame

Fabrication on hold. The next deliverable is a measured survey of the proposed supports on Earl, our 2007 Thomas HDX. The mirrored rear portal is a model gap and has no approved chassis attachments.

The 36 × 8 ft dance deck concept has two edge girders, 13 floor joists and two transverse bearers. Four interior posts and two front portal shoes show proposed attachment locations. A provisional mirrored rear portal is now shown as a visual study, with two additional candidate rear feet whose actual mounting locations remain unknown. Their positions determine girder spans and rear overhang. View the model in the panel workshop.

Support survey

Use one datum for all proposed support points, including the two speculative rear portal feet. Record fore-aft distance from the front axle centerline, offset from the bus centerline and height from a documented level reference. At each point, locate both the deck-to-post joint and the foot on the bus. Identify the supporting member, measure accessible dimensions and clearances, and photograph both sides with a scale.
