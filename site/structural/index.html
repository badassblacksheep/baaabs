What Thomas tells us

Period drawings show a 28.75-inch module. The 130YS drawing also flags a false rafter. Later HDX specifications describe bows extending down the side panels and bolted body-to-chassis clips.

What we inferred

Use every fourth module as the starting pitch, align lower truss nodes with the shores, and center the group within our portal span. The drawing belongs to another bus; it supplies no rating for Earl’s landings.

What to verify on Earl

Identify full bows on both sides and trace their joints through the sidewall, floor crossmembers and mounts to the chassis. Inspect corrosion, door interruptions and service clearances. Thin roof sheet or a window stile alone is insufficient evidence.

Earl’s auction label reads 79765-0538548-130YS, build 11-2005, chassis model year 2007. The reference 130YS drawing is dated October 2006. These are related designs, not matching order drawings. Pair positions may need a common fore/aft shift; a ±14⅜ in shift was included in the calculation sensitivities below.

Calculation record

The math underneath.

3D Euler–Bernoulli beam members, rigid joints, compression-only vertical shore springs and assumed fixed chassis seats. At every solve the implementation checks applied crowd load, free-node equilibrium, total force and total moment balance.

Load accounting for the crowd selection

f = fframe+floor+shores + fskin + fgear + α N (225 lbf)

4,796.56 + 2,311.25 + 250 + 2 × 20 × 225 = 16,357.81 lbf

11,858 lb physically carried in this model at 20 people. The multiplier adds force, not physical mass.

Steel, plywood and six shore/plate weights are included. Gear and skins are tested balanced and wholly on either side, smeared along the edge girders. The live-load envelope includes full deck, half deck and side-group footprints swept along the length. Local hangers, shelves, plates, welds, bolts and the real bus body are not modeled in detail. Unweighed guards, stairs, speakers and reinforcement remain outside this mass total.

Support law and stress check

K u = f + R(u),   Rᵢ = kᵢ max(0, −uᵢ),   kᵢ ≥ 0

Each spring acts at a lower-chord joint and carries compression only. No body hold-down or lateral restraint is credited. The full 3D frame still uses end portals; six shores do not replace them. Rear drop-frame transfer and seat fixity are assumptions.

σ = |Naxial| / A + |Mᵧ| h / (2 Iᵧ) + |M𝓏| b / (2 I𝓏) ≤ 0.60 × 345 = 207 MPa

Governing baseline: rear transverse sill; rear crowd with driver-side skins.

This is a chosen normal-stress screen, not a complete code strength check. Buckling, shear, torsion, joint eccentricity, welds, fatigue, lateral loads, chassis twist and body strength need separate analysis. Dynamics would require M ü + C u̇ + K u = f(t); natural frequencies, damping and rhythmic response are not solved here.
