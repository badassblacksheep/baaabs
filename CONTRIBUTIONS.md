# BAAABS contributions

A restrained “Help build BAAABS” link appears beside the hero’s 3D viewer button. The contribution section at `/#contribute` also links directly to the public fundraiser:
https://www.gofundme.com/f/help-build-baaabs-a-black-sheep-bound-for-burning-man

The fundraiser was verified on October 16, 2026 as “Help Build BAAABS: A Black Sheep Bound for Burning Man,” organized by Skip Lester. Use the public `/f/` address, never the organizer’s `/manage/` address.

Visitors choose their amount and complete payment on GoFundMe. The link opens in a new tab and works without JavaScript. The site does not collect payment details, set a donation amount, claim completed payments, or display fundraising totals.

The existing private ownership, use-of-funds, and development disclosures remain. The previous unconfigured Stripe scripts are no longer loaded; they are retained only as historical setup files and do not control the active fundraiser link.

When changing the destination, verify the fundraiser title and organizer. Keep totals and payment confirmations on GoFundMe unless a separately verified integration is added.
